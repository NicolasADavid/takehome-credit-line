CREDIT LINE INCREASE TAKE-HOME: FILES AND FOLDERS ADDED
=======================================================

Everything below was added or changed on top of the provided starter repo.
Framework used: React Native (react_native_app/). The Flutter app, mock API,
design tokens and data are unchanged.


DECISIONS.pdf
-------------
The one-page ship decision. Ship the increase feature only to members with
headroom (max eligible limit above their starting limit, ~77% of members) and
cap the slider at that room; members already at their max keep the control
experience. Evidence: within every headroom band, which is fixed at signup
and so still randomized, members with headroom retained +5.9 to +6.3 pp
better at 30 days and +7.3 to +7.6 pp at 60 days, while members at their max
retained 18 to 26 pp worse. Expected effect: about +4.6 pp retention at 30
days and +5.7 pp at 60 days across all members. Also lists the two next
experiments.


analysis/
---------
Python scripts (standard library only) that produced the numbers in the
PDFs. Both read the CSVs one row at a time and keep only totals in memory.

  retention_by_group.py
      Retention by experiment group: open at snapshot, plus 7/14/30/60-day
      retention counting only members observed for the full window, with
      treatment-minus-control differences and 95% confidence intervals.
      Run: python3 analysis/retention_by_group.py

  requests_breakdown.py
      Joins requests.csv to users.csv: data integrity checks, request
      funnel, retention by request outcome, retention by headroom band
      (treatment vs. control), outcome by days since joining, approval rate
      by requested limit vs. max, and what happens after a decline.
      Run: python3 analysis/requests_breakdown.py


experiments/
------------
One PDF per next experiment. Page 1 explains the experiment (hypothesis,
background, arms, metrics, sample size, ship rule); page 2 shows screenshots
of each arm; page 3 explains how to try both arms in the app.

  experiment-1-pre-approved-offer.pdf
      Members with headroom. A = the shipped request flow with the slider
      capped at their room. B = a pre-approved offer with one-tap accept.

  experiment-2-at-max-message.pdf
      Members at their max. A = no Increase button (shipped). B = an honest
      "You're at your maximum limit" card, with no credit check or decline,
      showing available to spend.

  screenshots/
      The designs: screenshots of every screen in each arm, captured from
      the running app (e1-* for experiment 1, e2-* for experiment 2).


react_native_app/App.tsx  (changed)
-----------------------------------
All app changes are in this one file; there are no API changes.
  - Ship decision: the Increase button appears only for members with at
    least $100 of room (max_eligible_limit_cents - limit_cents), and the
    slider is capped at that room, so no request can be declined.
  - Experiment 1, arm B: PRE-APPROVED card on Home and an offer screen
    (OfferCard, OfferPage), defaulting to the full amount, with an option
    to choose a smaller amount.
  - Experiment 2, arm B: at-max card on Home (AtMaxCard).
  - An ARM A/B control on Home for demos. Its labels follow the persona's
    experiment, which is decided by headroom.
  - Both arms of experiment 1 share the capped slider (IncreaseSlider) and
    submit logic (useSubmitIncrease).

To try it:
  cd mock-api && npm start
  cd react_native_app && npm install && npm run web
Then use the PERSONA and ARM controls on Home. Restart the mock API to
reset requests.
